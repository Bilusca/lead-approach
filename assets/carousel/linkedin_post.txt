honest confession: i've always been super curious about marketing, but i absolutely despise cold calls.

like, the thought of picking up the phone to interrupt someone's day and pitch out of nowhere? instant awkwardness. i'm way too introverted for that.

and to be real, forcing busy people into a 30-minute zoom call just to explain what you do feels totally broken in 2026. nobody has time for another awkward meeting with someone they don't know. 

so, because i'm a builder, i decided to engineer my way out of this pain.

i spent the last few weeks testing an experiment: what if outbound was conducted 100% asynchronously over text (linkedin, dms, whatsapp), with zero pressure and zero calls?

instead of sliding in asking for their time, you interrupt with a genuine "give" — a 30-second technical diagnostic of where their mobile site or funnel is leaking potential clients. you share 2 actionable fixes right there in the chat, no strings attached.

it felt so much more natural (and had way higher reply rates) that i turned the entire system into an open-source tool called lead-approach.

it works with whatever agent you use (antigravity, claude code, codex), runs automated technical audits on websites in batch, and drafts personalized 4-block messages that don't sound like generic agency spam.

i put together a quick neo-brutalist breakdown of the whole flow in the slides above. 

it's completely free, open-source, and live on npm. you can run it right in your terminal:
npx lead-approach install --all

if you also get nervous about cold calling or just want a calmer, more respectful way to prospect for your projects, i hope this helps you out.
