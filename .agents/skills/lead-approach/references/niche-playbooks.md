## 1. Law Firms & Attorneys (Personal Injury, Immigration, Corporate & Estate)

### 📌 Typical Niche Diagnosis:
* **Common Digital Bottlenecks:**
  * Outdated, text-heavy desktop websites with slow mobile performance.
  * Cold, bureaucratic contact forms with 8 to 10 mandatory fields that mobile users abandon.
  * Lack of a direct, confidential 1-tap intake route via corporate WhatsApp or SMS.
* **Ethical Bar Sensitivity (Bar Association / OAB):**
  * Attorneys strictly avoid aggressive pitches or unsolicited cold calling.
  * The angle must focus strictly on **client convenience during urgent inquiries** and **frictionless confidential intake**.
